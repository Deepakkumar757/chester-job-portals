Chester has employment and industrial locations including Sealand Industrial Estate and Chester West Employment Park. These areas include industrial, commercial and warehousing uses, but not every site is easy to reach by public transport. Check the actual worksite, shift start/end and last bus/train before applying.

Application checks before you spend time

  • Filter to part-time and confirm contracted weekly hours. If your student immigration conditions limit term-time work, ensure the role fits those conditions.
  • Check the exact address and shift times against public transport. Early warehouse, late hospitality and overnight shifts may not be practical without a suitable commute.
  • Use the employer's official career page where possible. If a job board lists a role, verify it on the employer page before sharing sensitive information.
  • Try nearby areas only if travel is realistic: Broughton, Saltney, Deeside, Ellesmere Port, Wrexham and other Cheshire border locations can have different commute times.
  • “Part-time employer” means a plausible source to check, not a guarantee that it is currently recruiting or will hire every applicant.
